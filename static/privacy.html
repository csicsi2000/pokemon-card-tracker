Cardex privacy policy

Last updated 6 September 2026.

Cardex is a free, open-source Pokémon TCG collection tracker published at csicsi2000.github.io/pokemon-card-tracker. It runs entirely in your web browser. There is no Cardex server, no account system and no database operated by the developer: nobody other than you receives, stores or can see your collection data.

Who is responsible

Cardex is a hobby project maintained by its author, Yuan Qi Zheng. Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's issue tracker at github.com/csicsi2000/pokemon-card-tracker.

What information Cardex handles

  • Your collection data: the cards you record as owned, wanted or offered for trade, your lots, decks, folders, formats and notes. You enter all of this yourself.
  • Display preferences: how the app looks on this device (for example which view a list uses).
  • Google account email address: only if you connect Google Drive, and only so the app can show which account is connected.

Cardex does not collect names, addresses, payment details, precise location, device identifiers or browsing history, and it has no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. It never leaves the device unless you choose to export a backup file or to connect Google Drive. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it completely.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, Cardex uses Google's OAuth sign-in and asks for two permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — to create and edit only the files Cardex itself creates. Cardex writes one folder in your My Drive, named Cardex, containing cardex-data.json (your collection data) and cardex-readable.md (the same data as readable text). It reads these files back to merge changes made on your other devices. It cannot see, list or modify any other file in your Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email — to read the email address of the connected account, which is shown on the Settings page so you know which account is in use. It is stored in your browser only and is not sent anywhere else.

The sign-in token Google issues is kept in your browser's local storage so that you do not have to sign in again on every visit. It is sent only to Google's own APIs. Cardex's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: the data is used only to provide the sync feature you asked for, is never transferred to anyone else, and is never used for advertising.

You can disconnect on the Settings page at any time, which deletes the token from your browser. You can also revoke Cardex's access under Google Account → Connections, and delete the Cardex folder from your Drive to remove the synced copy.

Third-party services

Card names, set information, images and market prices come from TCGdex, an open card database. Loading them means your browser makes ordinary web requests to TCGdex's servers, which see your IP address like any website you visit. No collection data is sent to TCGdex. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which is subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing, selling and retention

Cardex does not share or sell any information, because it never has any: there is no server to hold it. Data stays on your device, and in your own Google Drive if you enable sync, for as long as you keep it there. Deleting it is entirely in your hands, as described above.

Children

Cardex is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no information from anyone.
